# Personas

**Power user**: on Env Hopper most working days, two or more visits a day, across about 24 pairs on 8 Environments. Knows exactly where they are going; wants the Version shown to be right without waiting.

**Regular user**: comes a day or two in a couple of months, to two or three pairs. Often lands from a link someone shared; wants the page to make sense on first sight.

Both visit briefly: one jump, often just to check what runs. Measured in [usage patterns](usage-patterns.md).
